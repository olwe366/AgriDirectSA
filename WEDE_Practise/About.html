Our Mission

AgriDirect SA was founded to build a fair, transparent, and efficient agricultural marketplace in South Africa. Our web platform connects emerging smallholder farmers directly with commercial buyer, ensuring fresher ingredients for kitchens and equitble profits for growers.

What Drives Us

Empowering Local Farmers

Smallholders growers often face steep distributor markups and limited access to formal buyers. We provide digital marketplace for transparent trading.

Streamlined Supply Chain

By shortning the supply chain from farm work to fork, produce reaches local kitchens qucker, cutting down food waste and guaranteeing peak quality.

Sustainable Local Economy

Keeping procurement within local farming communities helps strengthen South African regional food system and supports rural economic growth.

Join The Movement

Whenther you are a farmer looking to expand your buyer networ or a chef looking for fresh farm produces, we are here to support your supply chain.
